Emmy is a sales specialist and trainer whose career started in the air — years as cabin crew with Qatar Airways and Saudia, leading teams and handling every kind of person under pressure. From there, luxury retail floors at Louis Vuitton and Bottega Veneta, managing VIP relationships and consistently exceeding targets.

That path led into real estate — first as an agent, then as a Sales Training Manager for brokerages including Banke International, Nexus, RichKey and The Heart of Europe developer, building onboarding frameworks, training needs analyses, and full learning management systems now used to train agents across the UAE.

Today she works both sides of the business: selling directly to clients across Egypt and the UAE, and training the agents who do the same through EMYNAJ.
